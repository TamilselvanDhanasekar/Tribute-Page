Ratan Tata
1937-2024
"THE ONLY WAY TO WIN IS TO NOT BE AFRAID OF LOSING"
- RATAN TATA
Ratan Tata, born December 28, 1937, is a renowned Indian businessman and philanthropist. He led Tata Group's growth from $100 million to $100 billion.

Known for humility, integrity, and philanthropy, Tata donated over 60% of his wealth to charity. He received the Padma Vibhushan and Padma Bhushan awards.
Tata's legacy inspires entrepreneurs worldwide, showcasing the power of leadership, philanthropy, and business excellence.
